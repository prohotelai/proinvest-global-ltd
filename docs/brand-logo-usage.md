# PROINVEST GLOBAL LTD logo usage

Approved corporate identity assets:

- `/brand/proinvest-logo.svg` — primary horizontal logo for light backgrounds.
- `/brand/proinvest-logo-dark.svg` — reversed horizontal logo for dark backgrounds.
- `/brand/proinvest-mark.svg` — icon-only corporate mark.
- `/icon.svg` — browser/app icon source.

## Website placements

- Header: horizontal logo, rendered at 213 × 44 CSS pixels.
- Footer: reversed horizontal logo, rendered at 260 × 54 CSS pixels.
- Browser/app icon: square mark, scalable SVG; source viewBox 128 × 128.

Do not place the long tagline inside header/footer or favicon-sized artwork. Preserve clear space around the mark and do not recolor the primary navy/gold identity outside approved monochrome/reversed uses.

Primary navy: `#0B315F`
Accent gold: `#C58A24`
